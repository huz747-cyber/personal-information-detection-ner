**Audit Objective:** Rigorously verify the methodology, independence, mathematical precision, and adjudication records of the double-annotation quality control process.

## 1. Transparency on Annotator Independence (Downgrade of Evidence)

> [!WARNING]
> **Independent Human Verification Status: DOWNGRADED TO SIMULATED CONCORDANCE**
> 
> Inspection of `src/dataset_tools/build_dataset.py` (lines 971–997) reveals that the second annotator dataset (`data/collection/double_annotation/annotator2_annotations.jsonl`) was **not generated by a blind, independent human annotator**. 
> 
> Instead, it was constructed programmatically by taking a deterministic random sample of 150 canonical sentences and injecting synthetic variance on institutional entities:
> ```python
> if idx % 25 == 7 and e["label"] == "ORGANIZATION" and any(k in e["text"] for k in ["Hospital", "Universiti"]):
>     e_copy["label"] = "LOCATION"
> ```
> While this test fixture successfully validates that the agreement engine can detect, isolate, and log class disagreements, **it does not constitute genuine human double-annotation evidence**. 
> 
> In accordance with Phase 2.6.1 audit requirements, this metric is officially documented as **Algorithmic Consistency & Synthetic Dual-Annotator Verification**, and must not be misrepresented as independent human validation.

## 3. Token-Level Agreement & Cohen's Kappa ($\kappa$)

* **Total Token Count Aligned:** 3,300 tokens
* **Matching Tokens:** 3,297 tokens (**99.91% raw token agreement**)
* **Discrepant Tokens:** 3 tokens:
  - `Hospital`: `B-ORGANIZATION` (Ann 1) vs `B-LOCATION` (Ann 2)
  - `Kuala`: `I-ORGANIZATION` (Ann 1) vs `I-LOCATION` (Ann 2)
  - `Lumpur`: `I-ORGANIZATION` (Ann 1) vs `I-LOCATION` (Ann 2)

### Cohen's Kappa Formulation:
$$\kappa = \frac{P_o - P_e}{1 - P_e}$$
- Observed agreement $P_o = \frac{3297}{3300} = 0.99909$
- Expected chance agreement $P_e = \sum (p_{1,c} \times p_{2,c}) = 0.58582$
$$\kappa = \frac{0.99909 - 0.58582}{1 - 0.58582} = \frac{0.41327}{0.41418} \approx \mathbf{0.9978}$$
(Interpretation: Near-perfect statistical concordance on aligned tokens).
